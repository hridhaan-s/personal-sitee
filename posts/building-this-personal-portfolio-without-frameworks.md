I wanted a place where I could put everything I achieve, learn, and build — so I made this portfolio without using React or another framework.

## The Stack

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Keeping the site lightweight made it easier to understand, change, and deploy.

## Domain and Hosting

I used Vercel for hosting and connected my own domain. The goal was to keep the site responsive while still giving it a personality that felt like mine.

## Why I Built It This Way

Frameworks are great, but I wanted to understand what was actually happening underneath the page. Building the core experience with vanilla web technologies gave me a much better feel for the fundamentals.

That is also why I am keeping the blog in Markdown: writing a new post should be as simple as creating a file, adding a little metadata, and committing it.
