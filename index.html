About

Hello!

I'm Hridhaan Sahay, a high-school student interested in cybersecurity, space, and technology. I explore those interests through coding, competitive programming, research, astrophotography, hackathons, and building projects.

I created BitBuzz, a student-run platform around innovation, space, and technology. I've also built projects for school, communities, and just because I wanted to see if I could.

When I'm not at my laptop, I'm usually looking up at the sky through my telescope.
