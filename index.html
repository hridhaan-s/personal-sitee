01 — PROFILE

I don't want to just use technology. I want to understand it, question it, and build with it.

I'm Hridhaan Sahay, a high-school student obsessed with how systems work — from secure digital infrastructure to the physics and scale of space.

I built BitBuzz to make science and technology easier to discover without the noise, and I keep building smaller experiments around cyber safety, AI, automation, and engineering.

Outside code, I look up. Astrophotography gives me a different kind of debugging: patience, observation, and thousands of tiny signals becoming one image.

Based in India · learning in public · building quietly.
