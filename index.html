01 PROFILE

A little context

I like hard problems and systems that make sense.

I'm a high-school student who likes understanding how things work — then pushing that understanding into something real.

I build web products, experiment with AI and automation, learn about cyber safety, and run BitBuzz, a student-led platform around science and technology.

When I'm away from a screen, I'm usually thinking about space. Astrophotography is my reminder that good work needs observation, patience, and a ridiculous amount of iteration.

“Build it. Break it. Understand why. Build it better.”
